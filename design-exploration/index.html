Working index

Open prototype

A useful directory, with the engineer close at hand.

The idea Harry’s collection of personally useful tools becomes a searchable public index.

Try it Search the project index by name, purpose, or category.

References, palette, and tradeoffs
Type
Arial throughout; compact titles, generous line spacing.
Composition
A calm introduction above a searchable six-project index and a strip of real interfaces.
Strongest at
Fast to scan; puts engineering substance before presentation.
Tradeoff
The least expressive option. The work has to carry the personality.

A personal letter

Open prototype

A personal journal, organised around the questions behind the projects.

The idea Harry’s account of engineering, fatherhood, and making becomes a personal letter with working links.

Try it Open a question to reveal the project behind it; follow its story or unfold the complete gitrove case study.

References, palette, and tradeoffs
Type
Georgia prose and questions, with small Arial project labels. Warm paper and muted red links.
Composition
An intimate opening letter flows into six expandable project questions, then pictures and longer notes.
Strongest at
A personal voice and a useful archive; the questions make the projects approachable.
Tradeoff
More reading, with project previews behind native disclosures. A separate picture collection supports visual browsing.

Slow Victory

Signal press

Open prototype

A software portfolio with the force of a printed poster.

The idea Harry’s name and the names of his own tools become the typography of a software poster.

Try it Filter the project roll by all work, developer tools, or home projects.

References, palette, and tradeoffs
Type
Impact display lettering, with Arial text. Flat colour and tightly set type.
Composition
A large two-line name on an acid-yellow field, then oversized project names interrupted by real screens.
Strongest at
Most assertive; memorable at a glance and distinct from a typical engineering portfolio.
Tradeoff
The poster voice is louder than the quiet personal practice. It needs restraint in the case studies.

Symbols and Pixels

Dot matrix

Open prototype

A software portfolio drawn as a living print specimen.

The idea Harry’s initials and his real product screens become a language of dots, pixels, and readable software.

Try it Switch actual project interfaces between dot-matrix prints and their original screenshots.

References, palette, and tradeoffs
Type
Arial throughout; a custom bitmap monogram carries the expressive role.
Composition
A wide two-part opening with a large raster identity specimen, followed by paired product prints and a quiet case-study reading surface.
Strongest at
Playful and tactile without relying on retro window chrome; the visual language is made from the person and the software.
Tradeoff
Halftone images communicate character rather than interface detail; the original-screen switch is essential for close reading.

Minimal Data

Project atlas

Open prototype

An engineering practice, seen through its actual materials.

The idea Harry’s six real projects and their listed technologies become a readable project atlas.

Try it Choose a technology to highlight the matching projects and read an exact count from the six-project inventory.

References, palette, and tradeoffs
Type
Arial throughout; large names, compact tabular figures, white-on-cobalt data.
Composition
A stable introductory column beside a large incidence matrix, followed by real interface images and a full project reading panel.
Strongest at
Makes the engineering practice tangible and explorable, using real relationships rather than decorative charts.
Tradeoff
More analytical than the personal letter. Five selected technologies are a useful lens, not a complete list of everything each project uses.
