The Hershey fonts
-----------------
Symbols, punctuation and some letter skeletons derive from the Hershey fonts. Their terms ask
that this acknowledgement travels with the data:

  The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S.
  National Bureau of Standards. The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally
  created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821
  (mit-eddie!ci-dandelion!hurt).

The data may be converted into any format except the original U.S. NTIS one. This font is not in
that format.

Drawn by If This Finds You
--------------------------
Letter forms drawn for the project after real traditions, released with the font under the same
licence:

- after Dr. A. V. Hershey (NBS, 1967)
- after continental European school hands (DE, AT, CH, FR, IT, ES)
- after 20th-century school print hands (hand-traditions.md §4)
- derived from this style's own 7 (Present-day adult print handwriting); after continental
  European school hands
- after the Palmer Method (1915) and Zaner-Bloser (c.1923) business hands
- after present-day adult hands (Copiste d’un jour, CREMMA; hand-traditions.md §4)
- Accented and special letters: Composed by If This Finds You from each hand's own letters,
  following the writing conventions collected in the project's research notes
  (hand-traditions.md §5).
- drawn to Hershey proportions
- hand-authored; conventions in docs/research/hand-traditions.md §5
- (hand-authored; conventions in docs/research/hand-traditions.md §5); umlaut as dots, ticks or
  a dash; ß: COERLL German pronunciation guide; BYU Script Tutorial
- after the handwritten en dash
- after the handwritten em dash

Full source list and how each one shaped the project: the reference library (docs/ in the
project repository).
