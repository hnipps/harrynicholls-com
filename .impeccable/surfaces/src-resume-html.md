# Resume page

Scope: `src/resume.html`, an unlisted reading of Harry's current resume. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: a recruiter Harry sends the link to. They read the resume on a phone or a laptop and can download the PDF. The page is not linked from the home page, the footer, or any other page, and it asks crawlers not to index it.

Content, in the PDF's order: name, title, location and addresses, summary, skills, experience, projects, education. Every sentence is the PDF's. A Download PDF link sits with the addresses. The theme switch and the way home are the site's own, not new resume copy.

Constraints: static HTML beside the other pages. No sitemap entry. `noindex, nofollow`. The PDF file is the one Harry attached.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The same well-made page as the home page, holding the full resume. One column, the name in the serif, the record in the sans. It refuses a second visual system, a card grid, and skill badges.

OWN-WORLD: The landing page's system, unchanged. Blue ink on near-white paper, a red mark for underlines and list markers, pale blue on navy at night. Instrument Serif for the name, Hyperlegible Sans for everything else, italic for section heads and the short role asides. Section heads hang in the left margin on a wide screen. No cards, no boxes, no new icons.

STORY: The reader sees who Harry is and where he has worked, in his resume's own words, and can take the PDF with them. The way back to the site is the footer, not a listing of this page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name at display size, outdented on wide screens, with the dot of the "i" as the light and dark switch. Under it, in the text column: the title, the place, then the same icon row as the home page for email, LinkedIn, GitHub, the site and the PDF. The summary follows. Skills begins as the first section. Project source links use that same icon button.
