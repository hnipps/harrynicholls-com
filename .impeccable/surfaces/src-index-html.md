## Direction contract

THESIS: A letter, then a log. The page is a signed letter whose postscript is a numbered log of questions. It refuses the developer-portfolio arrangement of headshot, skills grid and project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Photocopy-grey paper with no chroma, ink at near-black, hairline ink rules, square corners. Hyperlegible Sans Bold in tight caps says things; Space Grotesk Medium in tracked caps with slashed zeros counts things; a felt-tip hand in Signal red-orange signs them. Hazard acid appears only as a highlighter behind links. Track cobalt is the focus ring. One index card with masking tape, tilted.

STORY: The visitor reads who Harry is, sees that each project began as a plain question, opens one, and finds the reasoning behind it. They believe he builds things carefully and for real use. They write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline nav with the spiral and name left, "Write to me" right. The greeting fills the width in bold caps on two lines at roughly a fifth of the viewport height. The letter sits in a narrower column pushed right, its first paragraph larger. The signature "Harry" in the hand, Signal, tilted, closes the fold. The primary action, the first log row, begins directly under the signature.

FORM: Letter with a log (the confirmed shape brief, not a rolled concept; no seed key, direction pinned by the user). Signature interaction: a row opens and its story wipes in left to right, the direction the spiral unwinds, on one axis with no bounce; the plus mark closes to a bar.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
