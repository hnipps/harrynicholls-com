# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People sizing Harry up professionally: hiring managers, engineers and possible collaborators. They arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn or GitHub, usually on a laptop at a desk, sometimes on a phone. They give the page about ten seconds to decide whether to keep reading.

## Product Purpose

harrynicholls.com is Harry Nicholls's portfolio. It introduces him in a short letter and lets a visitor open the story behind each of six projects. Success is a visitor who knows who he is, opens at least one story, and knows how to write to him.

It is not the Slow Victory store. Shirts, art and writing stay off this site until they exist. The site borrows Slow Victory's type, palette and one mark, not its name.

## Brand Personality

Three words: sincere, mechanical, homemade.

Voice: first person, plain, short. Every project begins with a question Harry actually asked ("What should we watch tonight?"). Jokes point inward. The page is signed.

Emotional goals: the calm of an instrument that works, the warmth of a note left on a desk.

## Anti-references

- The paper stack this page replaces: peel corners, curled sheets, six themed views.
- Cream and beige "artisanal" paper.
- Developer-portfolio defaults: hero headshot, skills grid, logo wall, "Hire me" banner.
- Terminal-cosplay dark mode: mono type on black as the whole identity.
- Gradient text, glass cards, glow effects.

## Design Principles

1. A letter, then a log. The person comes first, then the work, in one column of attention.
2. Questions lead. A project is named by the question that started it; its name is the readout.
3. The hand is rare. Handwriting appears for the signature and the sign-off, and for one sincere sentence inside a story. Never more than that.
4. Sincere words, loud surfaces. Copy stays plain; type and colour carry the noise.
5. Works with nothing switched on. Stories open without JavaScript, and every old link still lands.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: body text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, visible focus, keyboard reachable. Signal and Track have separate text tokens that change on dark. Every animation has a reduced-motion alternative. Handwriting is never the only place a fact appears.

## Source of the visual system

Slow Victory toolkit v0.4 (`command-centre/brand/slow-victory/`): Hyperlegible Sans for display and body, Space Grotesk for labels, the Casimir Dunmore hand, the photocopy-grey palette with Signal, Track and Hazard, and the spiral mark.
